# Home

**MindMap** is a tool that turns your YouTube viewing history into a living document of your intellectual and emotional patterns.

## Why?

We spend hours on YouTube, yet we rarely reflect on what our viewing says about us. MindMap provides a mirror—not judgment, but insight.

## Core Philosophy

- **Privacy-first** – all processing is local (standalone) or on your own server.
- **AI-augmented, not AI-reliant** – you can use DeepSeek for richer analysis, but the core metrics work offline.
- **Actionable** – the dashboard helps you understand your content diet, curiosity profile, and even mental health signals.

## How It Works

1. Export your YouTube history (JSON) from Google Takeout.
2. Upload to MindMap (either the HTML client or via the API).
3. Explore the dashboard: categories, channels, viewing times, psychometric indicators.
4. Optionally connect to a VPS backend for transcript-level analysis (synopsis, tone, topics).

## Use Cases

- **Personal insight** – discover your dominant interests and viewing habits.
- **Educational** – use as a data viz project to understand API usage and analytics.
- **Research** – aggregate and analyse YouTube viewing behaviour at scale (with consent).
